The Case For Consuming Better

Eco-friendly = Human-friendly

Roots of the Project

Health and climate change go hand in hand. Eco-friendly products are human-friendly; polluting products are also human polluting— the health of people and of the natural Earth are intertwined, but there is a deep fracture here. We fail to realise this interconnectedness in the way that we consume.

A couple of summers ago I learned what “forever chemicals”* are, it happened to be the same summer that I had a big health intervention and was constantly in and out of the doctor’s office. Being in such a sensitive state and surrounded by the ever-sickening American food system made the links between food, health, and climate change clearer than ever. The same way that humans are depleting the Earth of her natural resources past the threshold of many points of regeneration, our bodies with immune systems idealized to heal ourselves, cannot keep up with the toxins building up from plastics in our clothes, pesticides in our food, and forever chemicals in the everyday products that we use. “The Case for Consuming Better” came out of a will to convince my family and my boyfriend to start thinking harder about the food that they eat and the products that they use everyday. It started with me, as I learn to consume more intentionally, but it is something hopefully everyone can take something from.

* Forever chemicals: chemicals found in most everything produced and consumed in the world that get absorbed into everything including our own bodies and never break down. The same way that they are not biodegradable, and will remain in the atmosphere forever, they can get into our bodies and can affect our health over time.
